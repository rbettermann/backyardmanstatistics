Format & rules
Every 2 hours
Each loop starts exactly every two hours on the bell, no matter how fast you were. The time left until the next bell is your rest; if it isn't enough to make the next start, you're out.
500 m swim
Fixed lane per athlete, roundabout (keep-right) principle. Wetsuit allowed, not mandatory; lifeguards at the pool the whole time.
20 km bike
Two 10 km laps along the Main, flat and fast. Helmet mandatory, no drafting. In darkness: front/rear light and a reflective vest.
5 km run
One lap along the water back to camp. Bib visible at the front. In darkness: headlamp and reflective vest.

Distances are swim + bike + run combined (25.5 km per loop).
